WHAT SHELFIE DOES TODAY
A mobile-first Next.js/TypeScript app collects name, email and mode preferences, verifies email, accepts photos of ONE product, rejects uncertain label reads, explains ingredients and shows a saved report. PostgreSQL enforces one completed free report per email across app instances. Failed reads and retakes do not consume it. Reopening retrieves the same report.

RULES
First report free, no payment or cancellation trial. Name/email precede scanning. Both Pregnancy & nursing and GLP-1 sensitive-skin modes change flags. Never guess label text or ingredient explanations. Separate drying alcohols from moisturizing fatty alcohols. Cosmetic information only; no diagnosis, treatment, cure, guaranteed outcome or safe/unsafe product verdict. Mode flags say commonly flagged, check with your doctor. Keep the navy/gold/ivory look, Cormorant Garamond and Jost fonts.
